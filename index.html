Why AuthentiMed

Screening designed for real-world access

Our goal is to explore a simpler first-line screening option for teams that may not have easy access to advanced analytical equipment.

Designed with affordability in mind

The concept aims to make preliminary screening more accessible than relying on high-cost analytical equipment for every initial check.

Portable by design

A compact device concept intended to bring a preliminary screening workflow closer to pharmacies and other medicine-handling settings.

Pattern-based alerts

Spectral measurements and AI comparison are intended to flag unusual results for review, rather than make an unsupported claim of certainty.

The proposed workflow

A first-line screening process

AuthentiMed is under development. The following describes the intended approach, subject to technical validation.

STEP 01

Capture a spectral pattern

A sensor records a spectral response from the medicine under controlled measurement conditions.

STEP 02

Compare with references

Software compares the measured pattern with reference data and looks for notable differences.

STEP 03

Flag for further review

An unusual pattern can prompt additional checks or certified laboratory testing where appropriate.
